Privacy Policy

Autonomous YouTube Studio · effective 29 October 2026

Autonomous YouTube Studio ("the tool") is private software used by its owner to publish and manage videos on YouTube channels that he owns. It has no other users. This policy explains what the tool accesses through Google's APIs and what it does with that data.

What the tool accesses

When the owner signs in with Google and grants access to one of his channels, the tool uses the YouTube Data API and the YouTube Analytics API (scopes youtube.force-ssl, youtube.readonly and yt-analytics.readonly) to:

  • read the authorised channel's identity (channel ID and title) to confirm it is the intended channel;
  • upload videos to that channel and set their titles, descriptions, captions, thumbnails, privacy status and publication schedule;
  • update the channel's own branding settings (description, keywords, banner);
  • read that channel's own analytics reports.

How the data is used

Data obtained from Google APIs is used only to publish and manage the owner's own videos and to report their performance to the owner. It is not sold, not used for advertising, not shared with or transferred to any third party, and not used to train artificial-intelligence models. The owner may use software tools, including AI assistants, to analyse his own channels' reports for himself.

Autonomous YouTube Studio's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Storage and security

Access tokens are kept in the operating system's encrypted credential store on the owner's computer and are never written to files or logs. Analytics and upload records are stored on the owner's own systems for his reporting. No user data is stored on this website.

Retention, revocation and deletion

Access can be withdrawn at any time from the Google Account's third-party access settings. After access is withdrawn the tool can no longer reach the channel, and stored tokens are deleted. Stored analytics and upload records are deleted within 30 days of a request or of the access being withdrawn.

YouTube

The tool uses YouTube API Services. By authorising it you also agree to the YouTube Terms of Service; Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
