Autonomous YouTube Studio

A private publishing tool.

Autonomous YouTube Studio is software that its owner uses to publish the educational videos he produces to YouTube channels that he owns. With his authorisation it uploads those videos, sets their titles, descriptions, captions, thumbnails and publication schedules, and reads the owner's own channel analytics so he can see how the videos perform.

The tool is not offered to the public, has no other users, and does not access any account other than the owner's own channels.
