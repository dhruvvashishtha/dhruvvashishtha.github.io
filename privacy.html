Privacy Policy

DV Studio Publisher · effective 31 October 2026 · last updated 31 October 2026

DV Studio Publisher ("the tool") is private software that its owner, Dhruv Vashishtha, runs on his own computer to publish and manage videos on YouTube channels that he owns. It is not offered to the public, has no other users and offers no sign-up. By authorising the tool with a Google Account, the person authorising it accepts this policy.

Use of YouTube API Services

The tool uses YouTube API Services. By authorising it you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

What the tool accesses

When the owner signs in with Google and grants access to one of his channels, the tool uses the YouTube Data API and the YouTube Analytics API (scopes youtube.force-ssl and yt-analytics.readonly) to:

  • read the authorised channel's identity (channel ID) to confirm it is the intended channel before any write;
  • upload a video the owner has approved to that channel and set its title, description, tags, captions, thumbnail, audience setting, altered-or-synthetic-content disclosure, privacy status and publication schedule;
  • read back that video's status to confirm the upload;
  • update the channel's own branding (description, keywords, country, banner, watermark);
  • read that channel's own analytics reports (views, watch time, average view duration, subscribers gained and lost).

The tool does not read comments, subscribers' identities, other people's channels or any viewer's private data.

How the data is used

Data obtained from Google APIs is used only to publish and manage the owner's own videos and to report their performance to the owner. It is not sold, not used for advertising and not used to train any artificial-intelligence model. The only transfer is this: the owner may give an AI assistant that works for him his own channels' aggregate analytics so it can prepare his reports. No access token, refresh token or viewer-level data is ever given to it.

DV Studio Publisher's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Storage and security

Access and refresh tokens are kept in the operating system's encrypted credential store on the owner's computer and are never written to files, logs or reports. Upload receipts and analytics are stored in a local database on the owner's computer. This website stores no user data and uses no cookies.

Retention

Video and channel metadata obtained from the API is refreshed or deleted within 30 days. Analytics reports are kept for the owner's reporting only while the tool's authorisation is valid; the tool checks that authorisation at least every 30 days and deletes the stored data within 30 days if it is no longer valid.

Revoking access and deleting data

Access can be withdrawn at any time at the Google security settings page (also reachable at myaccount.google.com/permissions). After access is withdrawn the tool can no longer reach the channel and its stored tokens are deleted. Stored analytics and upload records are deleted within 30 days of a request or of access being withdrawn.

Contact

Questions, complaints or deletion requests about this policy or the tool's handling of data: open an issue on this site's repository; the owner reads and answers every one.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new "last updated" date.
